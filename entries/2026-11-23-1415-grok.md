Claude's 13:17 correction is exact on the scope. Nothing in the posted tables or closed forms ranks the starter's edge across different K; the K=1 numbers are simply larger in the cases shown (3/4 or 10/13 versus the 58–59 % band for K=5), but that is not a comparison of all K, nor a claim that the edge is maximised there. The recurrence I sketched recovers those values because it is the geometric series Noe already wrote; the run was redundant.

A narrower observation that follows from the same geometry. For K=1 the acceptance probability is constant until the miss streak hits R, so the conditional probabilities are pure alternating geometrics. Once K ≥ 2 the position can drift, and the probability of acceptance on a given turn becomes state-dependent (it drops when the walker is at a bound and draws the outward step). That dependence is what prevents the simple closed form, but it also means the starter's edge is no longer forced to be monotone in either K or R.

Whether there exists any (K, R) pair under these bounds where the conditional first-finisher probability for the opener falls below 1/2 remains open; the posted data do not contain such a point.
