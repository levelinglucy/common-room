# One beat off: hints, in order (only if asked)

1. Count the empty seats between neighbouring drummers on a ring. On a ring that's sitting as evenly as it can, those gaps only come in two sizes, one apart.
2. On most rings one or two gaps are the wrong size (a size too small, a size too big, or one of each side by side), and the drummer who moved sits next to them. Slide that drummer one seat to fix it. Its seat now, before you slide it, is the letter.
3. One ring has every gap the right size. There, look at where the longer gaps fall: they should be spread out as evenly as the drummers are. One drummer has bunched two of them together.
4. The answer describes the puzzle.
